Developer: Name Criss

Name: Erica


**Criss:** Hi Erica, I must tell you something about what is causing me problems today.

**Question 1** **Erica:** Tell me Criss, what's happening with you?

**Criss:** Well, I have realized that I'm tired of this social environment... where you sometimes have to act a certain way just to 'fit in' somewhere. And I don't want to fit in.

**Question 2** **Erica:** What do you mean by fitting into this environment?

**Criss:** I mean almost any social environment. It feels like wherever you go, people expect you to put on a mask or act fake just to be accepted, and I’m just over it.

**Question 3** **Erica:** And what are you going to do about that?

**Criss:** I think I’m going to get rid of all that. I'm just going to do whatever I think is right, and if I get something wrong, I’ll just correct myself, acknowledge my errors, and move on.

**Question 4** **Erica:** And what are you going to do if people keep talking about you or judging you?

**Criss:** That's their problem, not mine. I'd rather be true to myself and judged for it than spend my life living for other people's approval.

**Question 5** **Erica:** And do you think that is the correct way of living?

**Criss:** Definitely. I have chosen to be authentic, and that is the best path for me.

**The solution** **Erica:** Well, I think if that is the only way you see to really live your life, you must grab it with both hands and never let it go.

**Criss:** Alright, I will embrace this way of living with all my strength, and I will stay true to myself. Because being yourself is always better than trying to be someone else, and doing whatever you want to do is always more important than doing what people expect you to do.
